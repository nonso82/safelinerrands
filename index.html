About Safeline

Safeline Errand & Logistics Services is a Nnewi-based errand, delivery and dispatch service. We give busy people, families and businesses a trustworthy way to get things done without leaving home or the office.

Every request is confirmed by our team, quoted transparently and completed by an assigned rider. No account needed, no surprise pricing.
